What it does

01Read

Opens fast and remembers where you were.

  • Search inside any document
  • Fill in forms, draw and highlight
  • Read aloud, print, share a page
  • Bookmarks, page grid, night display

02Scan

Point the camera at a page. The edges are found for you.

  • Colour, grey or crisp black and white
  • Text recognised on the phone, offline
  • Latin and Cyrillic, both built in
  • Scans you can search and copy from

03Fix

Every tool works on your phone and never uploads a thing.

  • Sign with your own signature
  • Merge, split, reorder, rotate
  • Compress, add page numbers, redact
  • Passwords, notes, images to PDF

Why it exists

Every free PDF app is full of ads. Every clean one wants a subscription.

Plain PDF is the third option. It is free and stays free. It does not hold the permission to use the internet, so it cannot show ads, cannot track you and cannot send your documents anywhere. Not "we promise not to". It is not able to.

The source code is published under the GPL, which means anyone can check these claims, and anyone who copies the app must keep it just as open. That is deliberate: the app was made to be passed along, improved and shared, so that help given once keeps on helping. It is built for people who want big buttons and no hidden gestures.

Languages

Seventeen of them, chosen inside the app.

English, Serbian, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Polish, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Hindi and Arabic. Text recognition reads English and Serbian out of the box, in Latin and Cyrillic script; other languages can be added.
